Analyst: Eli Talbert
Paper ID: ODMK
Date: 2026-10-24

Focal result

Paper ID ODMK: Kim, H.-S., Kim, H. J., and Juon, H.-S. (2021), “Racial/Ethnic Disparities in Patient-Provider Communication and the Role of E-Health Use.” The assigned result is in Table 2, PPPCQ column, Asian and non-Hispanic White rows: Asian mean 3.11 (SE 0.10; 95% CI 2.90–3.33) versus non-Hispanic White mean 3.45 (SE 0.02; 95% CI 3.39–3.49). The article reports lower PPPCQ among Asian respondents.

Analysis that produces the focal result

The unit is a HINTS respondent, and the population is adults in the 2018 HINTS 5 Cycle 2 patient-provider communication module. PPPCQ is the equal-weight mean of seven reverse-scored 1–4 communication items; higher values indicate better communication. The focal predictor is race/ethnicity, comparing Asian with non-Hispanic White respondents. Table 2 is unadjusted, so no covariates enter the analysis. The baseline estimator is the PERSON_FINWT0-weighted mean for each group. Uncertainty uses all 50 JK1 replicate weights, coefficient 0.98, and 49 denominator degrees of freedom. The reported direction is lower PPPCQ among Asian respondents.

Focal estimate

Each specification will report both weighted PPPCQ means and their direct difference (Asian minus non-Hispanic White) on the 1–4 scale. A negative value indicates a lower Asian mean. The reconstructed baseline difference is −0.3296 (JK1 SE 0.1097; 95% CI −0.5502 to −0.1091). The article did not print this direct difference.

4. Dimensions varied and their options

d01 — Analytic sample construction

Original option: orig_content

What it does: Include respondents with FreqGoProvider in 1–6, or with at least one valid communication response when FreqGoProvider is missing. The article is silent; this reading is consistent with NCI leaving a missing filter unreplaced when a follow-up was answered, and it reproduces Table 2.

Alternative option: alt_filter

What it does: Require an observed positive routing response (FreqGoProvider in 1–6); do not use answered follow-ups to resolve a missing routing field.

Why it is reasonable: Both rules target respondents reporting on provider communication in the prior 12 months. A researcher could reasonably follow the recorded routing response literally. Thirty complete-item respondents have a missing routing response, including three in the official Asian group.

d03 — Outcome operationalization

Original option: orig_7of7

What it does: Reverse-score and equally average the seven PPPCQ items only when all seven are valid. The article does not state a threshold; this rule exactly reproduces its Table 2 sample size and displayed estimates.

Alternative option: alt_4of7

What it does: When at least four of the same seven items are valid, reverse-score and equally average the available items. Do not impute missing items.

Why it is reasonable: A later validation study using these HINTS 5 Cycle 2 items calculated the seven-item score with at least four valid responses. This keeps the same domains and 1–4 scale while including respondents with four to six valid items.

d04 — Focal predictor operationalization

Original option: orig_official

What it does: Use the article’s mutually exclusive groups: Asian is RaceEthn5=4 and non-Hispanic White is RaceEthn5=1. Non-Hispanic multiracial respondents remain in Other.

Alternative option: alt_any_asian

What it does: Define non-Hispanic Asian as Hisp_Cat=10 plus at least one Asian checkbox (AsInd, Chinese, Filipino, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, or OthAsian), including multiracial selections. Keep White fixed at RaceEthn5=1.

Why it is reasonable: The survey permits multiple race selections. An inclusive any-Asian definition is a reasonable peer choice that retains the Asian-versus-non-Hispanic-White comparison. It adds 21 non-Hispanic multiracial Asian respondents in the source file.

d08 — Clustering, nesting, and standard errors

Original option: orig_jk1

What it does: Use PERSON_FINWT0 for point estimates and all 50 official replicate weights for JK1 variance, with coefficient 0.98 and 49 denominator degrees of freedom.

Alternative option: alt_taylor

What it does: Use Taylor linearization with PERSON_FINWT0, VAR_STRATUM, and VAR_CLUSTER, with 98 denominator degrees of freedom.

Why it is reasonable: NCI documents both methods for HINTS 5 Cycle 2. They target the same population and use the same point weight, so group means and their difference must be identical; only standard errors and confidence intervals may differ.

7. Notes

Four rather than five dimensions are varied because no defensible fifth choice preserves the same focal quantity without duplicating an included decision. d02 overlaps d03; adding controls, changing the outcome model, or dropping population weights would change the assigned quantity or target population.

The article does not state the valid-item threshold or its treatment of answered communication follow-ups when the routing response is missing. It also does not discuss multiracial Asian classification or print a direct Asian-minus-White contrast. The inferred baseline rules reproduce the Table 2 N=2,611 and displayed means.

Each survey design will be created from the full public-use file before analytic-domain restrictions. Only the published baseline and structural feasibility counts have been run; no proposed alternative result has been calculated.
