# Third-party materials

The repository licenses cover only original project code and project-authored
documentation. They do not grant rights in the materials listed below.

- **HINTS 5 Cycle 2 data and documentation:** distributed by the US National
  Cancer Institute through the [HINTS data portal](https://hints.cancer.gov/data/download-data.aspx).
  The respondent file and original download bundle are not tracked. Users must
  follow NCI's public-use and confidentiality guidance.
- **ODMK article:** Kim, Kim, and Juon (2021),
  <https://doi.org/10.1080/10810730.2021.1919248>. The article and its text
  extraction are not redistributed; the source ledger records the inspected
  copy's provenance and checksum.
- **COS materials:** the sourcing sheet, workflow, robustness guidance,
  preregistration template, and SCORE robustness supplement remain subject to
  their respective owners' terms. Their inclusion or citation does not place
  them under this repository's licenses. The SCORE supplement itself is not
  redistributed.
- **Moser et al. (2022):** the retained validation article is identified by
  DOI <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0279725> and carries its stated CC0
  dedication.
- **Software dependencies:** Python, R, and GitHub Actions dependencies retain
  their own licenses.

URLs, checksums, roles, and acquisition notes are recorded in
[`sources/source_manifest.csv`](sources/source_manifest.csv).
